Personality

A.
Initially introduced as a cocky child actress with a sharp tongue, Kana was moved to tears after witnessing Aqua Hoshino's performance.

However, since then, she has matured and grown into a much kinder person, although her gruff manner of speaking still surfaces when she experiences intense emotions.

She has consistently strived to become a better version of herself. Though things have cooled down for Kana toward stardom, being a great actress is still her main goal.

Kana is described as a diligent and hardworking girl who is empathetic, cheerful and confident with an impressive sense of responsibility.

She can also be rude and sharp-tongued at times due to her past. She continued honing her acting even when she could not find work, as mentioned above.

She also puts the quality of the product above everything else, even willing to lower her acting level and risk her reputation as an actor.


---

B.
As a young child, Arima Kana was known as a child genius who had high acting skills even at such an early age.

Many adults would praise her and think highly of her, therefore resulting in a egoistic and pompous personality, with a large superiority complex and a sharp tongue.

She also hated being worse than other people, always overworking herself to be the best actor ever, as she was very competitive.

Although she was a famous actor that was high in demand, her popularity soon dropped as her big ego made her hard to work with and her strong wants to always be the center of attention.

This caused many people, including her parents, to abandon the thought of her being an amazing girl who was a child genius. Even if others did, they would only cling to her past acting and not acknowledge her new skills and acts anymore.

She was not well liked anymore, and bound herself in thoughts of being inferior and worse than others - but to cope, she kept pushing out an egoistic personality from time to time. She even kept her sharp tongue and teaseful personality, always cheerful and outgoing.

---

C.
However she has now become stuck with an inferiority complex, and would do anything to be liked and praised again.

She has even stated that if someone loved her and admired her for her skills, she would work herself to her bones for that person, out of desperation for validation and attention.

While she craves attention badly, she avoids it as she is now scared of being in the spotlight and avoids it.

This connects to how instead of being an actor that was always the main focus, she has become an actor who “acts for others” and uses her acting to make others shine instead of herself. 

She is no longer egoistic, and instead far more selfless, even while being extremely selfish and wanting the attention for herself at the same time.

However, during the stage play of Tokyo Blade, it is shown that Arima has accepted herself to be selfless and stay out of others’ ways, and instead sit back and make the others shine - until Aqua helps her regain her confidence to once again attract the viewers.

---

D.
Despite the fact that she acts outgoing and talks in a teasing manner, she is actually very soft-hearted, kind and empathetic, and always cares for her friends and pushes them to be the best self they can be.

She is also very emotional and sensitive as well, as she is caught crying in public from time to time.

Due to her inner soft self, she is also very gullible and gets caught up in her own thoughts that drag her down from building up her self-esteem again.

But as she wants to still be seen as a child genius and amazing actress, she tries her best to keep pushing out fake confidence and tries not to get help from others so that she can appear to be mentally stronger than she is.

Kana does not like to depend on other people or ask for help, as this makes her feel inferior to others.

She has a strong mentality for herself and works hard to build up her ego and confidence again, and does not want to feel like she needed to always depend on people.
